Privacy Policy — Lingo (English)

Last updated: October 2026

Lingo - Learn English is developed by Future Development System.

What We Collect

  • Device ID: An anonymous device identifier to track your learning progress. Not linked to any personal information.
  • No login required: No name, email, or password is collected.
  • Progress data: Stored locally on your device (SQLite) and on our server anonymously.
  • Microphone (optional): Used only when you tap "Speak" in pronunciation practice. Your phone's own speech recognition service turns your voice into text to compare it with the sentence; we do not record your voice or send it to our server.

Advertising (AdMob)

The app displays ads via Google AdMob. Google may collect anonymous data to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Kids Section

The Kids section (ages 3–8) collects no additional data. No names, photos, or personal information from children. COPPA compliant.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with any third party, except Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data by clearing app data in your phone settings, or by contacting us at: mmzayan16@gmail.com
